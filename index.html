🐝 Our Process

How We Collect Honey

From deep Sundarban forests to your home — a journey of purity and care at every step.

Step 1

🌳 Collection

Our experienced beekeepers carefully collect raw honeycomb from natural bee hives deep within the Sundarban mangrove forests using traditional, bee-friendly methods.

Step 2

🔬 Filtering

The raw honey is gently filtered using fine mesh cloth to remove beeswax and debris while preserving all natural enzymes, pollen, and nutritional value.

Step 3

📦 Packaging

Each jar is carefully filled, sealed, and labeled in our hygienic facility. We use food-grade glass and BPA-free containers to maintain freshness.

Step 4

🚚 Delivery

Your honey is securely packed and shipped directly to your doorstep through our trusted delivery partners. Track your order in real-time via WhatsApp.
